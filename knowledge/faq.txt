DEVFORGE Internship FAQ

Q: What is DEVFORGE?
A: DEVFORGE is an internship program focused on AI Engineering and Full Stack Development.

Q: What technologies are used?
A: Python, FastAPI, LangChain, LangGraph, GitHub, Render and Ollama Cloud.

Q: What is the final submission?
A: GitHub Repository and Render Deployment Link.

Q: Is Render free?
A: Yes, Render provides a free web service for deployment.

Q: Should I upload my .env file?
A: Never upload your .env file.
